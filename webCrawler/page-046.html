Why is CETYS your best option?

01

We are among the best universities in Mexico.

CETYS is among the best private universities in Northwestern Mexico. It has been #1 in Baja California for more than 10 consecutive years.

* Ranking of the 100 best universities in Mexico according to “Selecciones” from Reader’s Digest.

View our Programs
02

10 different national and international organizations accredit the quality of our programs.

Only 5 universities in Mexico and 10 in Latin America are accredited by WASC, which also accredits Stanford, UC Berkeley, and UCLA.

See more
03

9 out of 10 of our students are employed within a year of graduation.

100% of them are consistently rated as “good or excellent” by their employers, and many have started their own businesses.

** 2021 Employment Study.

Explore the CETYS Alumni Network
04

We take our professors’ experience very seriously.

All full-time professors have a Master’s Degree and more than 70% have a Doctor’s Degree. 3 out of 10 are part of the National System of Researchers (SNI).

05

We provide the tools and academic support that help you develop your innovative ideas.

Our students’ projects participate in programs from organizations such as the UN and NASA, and are featured in national and international media.
